Cache construction and packet construction are separate from submission.
The main upload helper emits source-memory references; source lifetime and the
later submission/fence contract require the consumer chain. Repeated palette
processing occurs only on the observed first-load flag paths; these bodies do
not establish a per-frame redundant upload problem. Map section count35 is used
before a local16-slot fill/count pass, but invalid inputs or an overrun have not
been reproduced and no defect is claimed.
