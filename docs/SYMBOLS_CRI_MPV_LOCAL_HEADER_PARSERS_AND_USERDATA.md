Local readers assemble/refill big-endian windows from aligned addresses; logical source begins after four prefix bytes, but lookahead may exceed the chunk length. They generally rely on dispatch/stream contracts for prefix identity and readable bytes. Sequence/GOP finish by rounding down to whole consumed bytes while retaining remaining bit offset at350: `residual=bitOffset&7; consumed=prefetch+((bitOffset-residual+7)>>3)-8-original`. The splitter1407E0 receives that literalbytecount,thenvtable20publishes channel0prefix and1Creturns channel1suffix. This does not establish the original stream provider's concurrency or partial-chunk guarantees.
