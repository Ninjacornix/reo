## Results

- **Shared format/suboperations:** first TIM2 picture at+16/+128 according to filebyte5; TIM2 magic/version4/format1/single-picture vocabulary; and GS BITBLTBUF/TRXPOS/TRXREG/TRXDIR plusIMAGE transfer sequence are concretely comparable. These support narrow format/register meanings, not shared engines or wholeupload functions.
- **Transfer composition differs:** CVX `LoadToVram` constructs static GIF/DMA storage, appends TEXFLUSH and immediately submits it. Outbreak `0x18C8C0` builds caller-owned VIF DIRECTHL chunk chains with separate flush builder `0x18CD00`. CVX `Tim2_Image_Load` uses 32764 quadword transfer limits; Outbreak uses 0x70000-byte chunks. Queuing,IRQ choice,tag order,geometry and referenced-data lifetime cannot be erased from an optimization.
- **Synchronization differs:** CVX `SyncPath` manipulates D_PCR and waits DMA, VIF1, VU and GIF states without a localtimeout. Outbreak `0x113488` has a polling bitmask mode and a shared iteration-limited wait with failure diagnostics. Shared busy-bit masks do not justify dropping waits or replacing them with a generic host fence.
- **CVX-specific readback/format policy:** `StoreRenderTex` reads fixed 512x512 frame content into its constructed TIM2 and clears the screen. Its header writes TW/TH 8 despite dimensions 512; preserved source evidence is not repaired by inference. Palette loader uses `picture+HeaderSize+ImageSize` and only accepts RGB32 indexed4/8; its loop does not advance the palette pointer/destination. Outbreak locator has aligned,mip-dependent pointer rules. These are different API/layer contracts.
- `UncAddr` is a guest 32-bit mask/uncachedalias; `loadImage` starts the GIF DMA channel rather than decoding an image; `ClearVram` emits a fixed 16384-iteration transfer sequence. Their names do not establish portable host replacements. No source issue here was assumed to exist in Outbreak.

Owned evidence: **11 complete Outbreak bodies/781 matching instruction comments**, 25 omitted zero words, zero conflicts/nonzero omissions.
