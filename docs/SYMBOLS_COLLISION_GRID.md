The collection path and resolver share process-global arrays. A future optimization must preserve which triangles are admitted, exact edge/vertex comparisons, the six-contact limit, and the order of correction selection. Per-axis extrema are not interchangeable with summation or averaging every contact. None of these functions has a new measured performance attribution.

## Floor-query contract refined

Existing mapped entry0x617000 remains in [gameplay notes](SYMBOLS_GAMEPLAY.md). It explicitly passes vertexcount3 to the generic polygon-fan helper0x370C70,using worldX/Z projection. It skips triangles with planeYcoefficient<=0,computes Y=-(nx*X+nz*Z+d)/ny,admits onlyY<inputY+100,then selects the greatest admittedY. Type/maskoutputscome fromthechosenrecord;normaldefaults(0,1,0),typeoutputsFF/mask0 when no floor. Optionalmatrixconstructionuses0x371480oridentityfallback.

Floor and collision queries are candidates for measuring repeated cell lookup,list traversal and geometric math. Static connectivity cannot establish their cost in a slow room. Guestfloatconversion,wrappedunsignedbounds,trianglewinding,edgeinclusion,NaN/degeneratebehaviorandselectionordermustbe retained or explicitly admitted before replacing the routines with a host spatial query.
