The six constructors above share allocator alignment and transfer-header fields, but produce different layouts and lengths. The selector's owned descriptors point to these constructors; see [descriptor decision table](SYMBOLS_RENDER_PROGRAM_SELECTION.md). Their first argument's+4C is the selected descriptor, not an arbitrary optional pointer. Common matrix sources are scratch70003700 and70003F40, then globals294D40/294D00.

These are EE callable functions containing VU macro instructions. Their mapping does not mean embedded VU microprograms have been translated or admitted to an AOT path. VRSQRT, VWAITQ, masked lanes, guest alignment, matrix ordering and caller-preserved registers need validation before replacement. No timing or root-cause assertion is made.
