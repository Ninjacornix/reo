# Native resource compatibility and quantity operations

`ResourcePairs` supplies5B3E40/5B4020/5B3DD0 compatibility, the complete
5B3690 quantity dispatcher and5B3820/5B39A0/5B3920 primitives. It operates on
the canonical World, actor inventory, ITEM templates, SceneResources and
ResourceFrames. It owns no second inventory or descriptor table.

Compatibility retains signed table words, unsigned content comparison, first
match ordering and conditional output publication. A matching kind0 still
publishes its extra halfword. Null/same records return0. The character4 override
uses the first owner's actual storage slots and the owned709EF0 eleven-pair
table. Content199 needs positive quantity, but not activity; no quantity is
consumed by classification. Failing the special recipe falls back to ordinary
compatibility. The active-ID wrapper has its own activity admission.

The dispatcher admits positive signed-low16 amounts, active owned records and
the first template's exactC01/100000 flag gates. CompatibilityD/1 transfers
quantity;B/C swaps the ordered quantities. These are the source's numeric
codes, not guessed item categories. The two transfer bodies are independently
word-audited and compared even though their admitted algorithms agree.

Primitive operations retain signed-halfword wrapping, their different admission
from the dispatcher and same-record write/read ordering. Depletion requests
actual5B1AF0 semantics: the source record's self-ID selects the destruction
record, whose self-ID selects final deactivation. Primary and shared-secondary
instances retire through the existing actual owners. Inventory slots are not
removed or compacted here. Return3 means destruction was requested, even when
the selected destruction record is inactive or unowned.

Both compilers pass36,553,248 combined assertions each. New coverage includes
251,863 compatibility queries,1,572,864 quantity operations,24 actual primary/
secondary release cases and26 refusals. All five owned ITEM banks plus a
controlled flag/compatibility bank run. The full signed quantity, amount, result
kind and table-content domains are exercised, with aliases, null sources,
inactive descriptors, ownership, self-ID redirection and signed-output cases.
The complete special table is checked with active/inactive content199 and
positive/zero quantity. Accessed table extents are guarded at the actual read:
an unmatched final halfword is valid; its missing payload refuses on a match.
Both parent builds and five integration tests pass. Evidence is sealed in
`native_resource_pairs_qualification_v1.json`.

The original special admission can read before the primary actor array when
owner0 reaches its character checks. Native code explicitly refuses that path
rather than inventing the preceding memory's character. Required missing primary
actors and genuinely accessed table words outside the owned extent also refuse.
These are remaining admission/modernization gates, not reproduced crashes.
