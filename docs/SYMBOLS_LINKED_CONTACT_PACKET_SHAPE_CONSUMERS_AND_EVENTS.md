The linked pool previously called "visual descriptors" in [pool/attachment](SYMBOLS_LINKED_VISUAL_DESCRIPTOR_POOL_AND_ATTACHMENT.md) is a **contact packet pool**: these consumers pass the records to [actor region scans and pending response writes](SYMBOLS_ACTOR_CONTACT_PACKET_SCANNERS.md), and [contact identity restore](SYMBOLS_CONTACT_PROVENANCE_AND_NEAREST_SEGMENT.md). Successful scene contacts can additionally spawn visual effects and request sounds. That effect output does not make the packet itself a render-work record. Bank names/author asset labels remain unknown; resource-driven setup is not proof of a visual-only role.
