The copy fast path is selected only after rejecting backward-overlap and checking both alignment and a32-byte minimum. The8-byte loop is entered after that aligned path; arbitrary unaligned pointers remain on byte copying. Initial decrements are countdown representation, not proof of a zero-length underflow loop: the equality gate runs before the byte body.

The search’s packed128-bit and scalar64-bit detectors use replicated01/80 byte constants and XOR against replicated target, followed by a byte-resolution loop. Their presence supports the byte-search role without proving every emulator SIMD implementation. The byte fallback tests the current character before incrementing and treats target0 through its final equality selection. No surrounding allocator/page overread allowance is assumed.
