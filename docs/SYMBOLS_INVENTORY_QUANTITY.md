Static File #1 US research, 2026-11-04. This follows the selected-resource callers
in [actor resource transitions](SYMBOLS_ACTOR_RESOURCE_TRANSITIONS.md) and
[resource actions](SYMBOLS_ACTOR_RESOURCE_ACTIONS.md). Original identities are
recorded in [the central index](FUNCTION_INDEX.md). No game, build, replay,
attachment or live-memory inspection was performed.

Resource IDs below index sixty-byte records at0x3C5630. Byte0 is tested for
activity, byte2 is used as the record's own ID, halfword6 its content type,
signed halfword24 its quantity, halfword28 its owner and byte2B its template
selector. These are observed consumers; all original names remain candidates.
The template helpers0x5B4320/4380 were mapped separately.
