The allocator rounds to the next16-byte boundary even for an aligned request. Its relocation wrapper rounds before calling the allocator, so their composition adds another alignment unit. Those byte-count contracts may matter for capacity analysis, but no fragmentation or allocation failure was observed. The messagegroup6 null result is a separate static caller/callee mismatch; an execution and guest-memory contract would be needed to establish consequences.

Audit: `audit_note_words.py` verified 1,057 owned instruction words across 20 catalogue entries, with zero conflicts and zero omitted nonzero words; the 42 omitted words are zero/NOP padding or delay slots.
