The bridge-returned next certificate is passed to `6FCD40` before its pointer is reused by the serializer. This is a literal ordering fact; the selected bridge's ownership contract is needed before interpreting its lifetime. Encoder results and updated output cursors are not checked against queried lengths. There is no local chain cycle counter, certificate deduplication, negative encoder-length admission, or u24 overflow check. Allocation failure inside chain assembly branches directly to the common zero return without the normal chain-context cleanup. Reachability and consequences of these cases remain untested.
