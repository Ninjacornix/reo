Pushback and refill share the layout established in [stream lifecycle](SYMBOLS_LIBC_STREAM_LIFECYCLE.md): ordinary buffer cursor/count are preserved at38/3C while pushback owns cursor/count, then refill can restore them. The inline pushback area has exactlythree bytes; byte43 is separately used for unbuffered I/O. The queue helper explicitly clears descriptor fields after attempted output, so failure does not preserve a ready-to-retry queue by itself.

Float values here travel as software-double integer carriers, with sign handling and comparisons delegated to [software floating arithmetic](SYMBOLS_SOFTWARE_FLOAT_ARITHMETIC.md); this is distinct from native f12/f13 arithmetic. A complete body read of the adapter does not prove the larger103C78 or10D1E0 algorithms conform to printf/dtoa. All408 instruction comments match owned ELF words; aligned omitted words are zero. No measured buffering, parsing or formatting bottleneck is claimed.
