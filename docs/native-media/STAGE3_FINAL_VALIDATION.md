## Measured limits and later stages

The temporary game-renderer handoff misses 42 source pictures in the cold full movie and 49 in recovered playback: latest-picture replacement occurs before the next game borrow. Maximum fresh-handoff gaps are about 67.7 ms and ordinal jumps are at most two. All source pictures being published is not proof every picture appeared on screen. This is a measured rendering-interface follow-up for Stage 5; no stutter-free claim is made. The cold source publication maximum is 66.6394 ms; recovered playback peaks at 45.5996 ms.

WGC and process-loopback recordings contain coverage/timestamp gaps. Zero returned audio packet flags do not prove continuous recording. The checked anchors avoid those gaps. Physical scanout, speaker latency, subjective lip sync and unobserved intervals remain unmeasured. No timing correction, fabricated sample or source-frame truncation is used to satisfy a gate.

Historical incident internals remain unknown. V37's freshly observed outstanding-request spans are 1,466.9548 and 2,199.9560 ms; they are not physical ReadFile durations.
