- **CVFS main:136578:** both concrete owned provider tables leave method44 NULL; the optional selector4 route has no bound implementing provider. - **Five raw-global/empty operations:** main:130E88/13F248/1DB090/1F2AE0 and o2:683AB0. Their local behavior is complete, but no typed producer, consumer, registration or caller identifies the missing quantity/object/table schema. The scenario reset caller of683AB0 establishes when its forty-byte block is cleared, not what that block represents.
- **Three dormant/boundary leaves:** main:11707C/132FB0/132FC8. No owned direct-transfer or literal-pointer evidence supplies their purpose.11707C's architectural delay word belongs to the following catalogue entry and allocates its stack frame; an independent callable boundary is unproved. The other two tail into bare return/zero stubs without a typed consumer.

Repeating the same wrappers' disassembly does not fill these gaps. The next useful input is a matching implementation, owned symbol/type carrier, concrete provider registration or typed data consumer. Any later live investigation remains a separate task under the player's runtime constraints. Unknown functions are not assumed unused, harmless or removable.
